# Lunch Light ☀️

Finds the sunniest (or at least brightest) 20–30 min lunch-walk slot between 09:00 and 15:30 on weekdays, using your phone's GPS and four forecast models via Open-Meteo (DMI HARMONIE, MET Norway, DWD ICON, ECMWF).

- Top 5 start times per weekday with score, sun %, brightness, rain risk and confidence (model agreement)
- Low-sun warning + nearest open ground/water in the sun's direction (OpenStreetMap)
- One-tap logbook; after 8 walks, models are re-weighted by your observed accuracy (stored only on your phone)

Install: open the GitHub Pages URL in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Data: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (CC BY 4.0) · © OpenStreetMap contributors
